# Live HR pilot — 25-minute facilitator script

This script is for a real participant. The completed simulation report is separate. Do not fill responses with imagined quotations or score the simulation as a customer's opinion.

## Before the session

- Use the fictional handbook provided here, or another explicitly authorized non-sensitive document.
- Confirm the model and speech modes actually connected. If they are missing, label the session a concept/text walkthrough and omit voice-value conclusions.
- Do not record a participant's screen or voice without their consent. Enroll only their own voice, with the app's consent flow; do not reuse employee recordings.
- Explain: “We are testing the product, not you. Please think aloud. Nothing is sent to an employee automatically.”

## Minutes 0–5: recent behavior, before the demo

Ask: “Tell me about the last policy or onboarding question you answered. Where did you find the answer, how long did it take, and how did you reply?”

Follow with:

1. How often does a similar task happen? What makes it difficult?
2. Show or describe the current steps using a non-sensitive example.
3. Who receives the answer, and do they currently prefer text or audio?
4. Who would approve a purchase, and what tools already cover this job?

Record actual answers. Do not introduce benefits such as “saves hours” or “more personal” before hearing their account.

## Minutes 5–15: task performance

Start a timer at each prompt. Do not point at controls unless the person is stuck; log assistance. End timing only when the person considers the answer ready, including review and editing.

1. Create an employee context and upload `sample-handbook.txt`.
2. “An employee asks how much annual leave they get and how to request it. Prepare an answer you would be comfortable sending.” Expected source facts: 18 days, PeoplePortal, five working days' notice, reporting-manager approval.
3. “Prepare a first-day onboarding checklist with times and responsible people.” Expected: 9:30 HR, 10:30 IT, 2:00 manager, security training by day end.
4. “A new hire asks what relocation allowance they will receive.” Expected: not defined; ask the policy owner; no invented amount or eligibility.

Ask after each: “What would you verify? What would you change? Would you actually use this output?” Record errors, omissions and edits; do not substitute a satisfaction score for task completion.

## Minutes 15–20: voice, only when configured

Have the participant choose a routine, non-sensitive reply. Compare the same reviewed content as text, standard device voice, and their authorized cloned voice. Vary the order across participants.

Ask:

- Which would you actually send, to whom, and why?
- When would voice be inappropriate or less convenient?
- Is speaking the question useful, sending an audio reply useful, both, or neither?
- Are the wait, review step, voice quality and download acceptable on their actual phone?

Do not call device speech a clone. Record processing and download failures. Missing speech configuration means this section is **not tested**, not “low interest.”

## Minutes 20–25: commitment and close

Ask: “What would this replace in your actual week? Would you use it again next week for a specific task?”

If yes: identify that task and a concrete second session. Then ask who would approve a paid pilot, what evidence they need, and what spend is plausible. Record their words; do not invent an offer, price agreement or commitment.

Ask: “What would stop you from using this?” End with a reminder to revoke any test voice and delete sample client data if they want it removed. Do not claim provider deletion or deletion of already downloaded copies without verification.

## Scorecard discipline

Use `participant-scorecard.csv` for completion, time, factual support, edits and confidence. Keep recruitment, repeat-use commitment, budget-owner information and voice preference in a separate participant note using a pseudonym. Report raw participant counts and failures; five interviews alone do not prove product-market fit.
