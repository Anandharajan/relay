FICTIONAL TEST DATA - NORTHSTAR SERVICES - HR HANDBOOK
Version: 1 November 2026. For product testing only; not legal or HR advice.

ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Submit requests in PeoplePortal at least 5 working days before the leave begins. The reporting manager approves requests. No more than 5 unused days may carry forward, and carried days expire on 31 March. Sick leave is a separate policy and is not specified in this document.

ONBOARDING - BEFORE DAY ONE
The HR coordinator sends the welcome email and requests the signed employment agreement 3 working days before the start date. IT prepares the laptop and company account before the start date. Do not collect identity documents through chat; use the approved secure onboarding portal.

ONBOARDING - DAY ONE
At 9:30 AM the HR coordinator hosts the welcome orientation. At 10:30 AM IT leads account setup. At 2:00 PM the reporting manager explains the role, first-week goals and the buddy assignment. The new employee completes the security training by the end of day one.

REMOTE WORK
Employees may request up to 2 remote-work days per week, subject to reporting-manager approval. Customer-facing coverage must be maintained. Requests go through PeoplePortal before the start of the work week.

EXPENSES
Submit routine business expenses through ExpenseDesk within 30 calendar days of the expense. Attach the receipt and business purpose. The finance team reviews complete claims within 7 working days. This policy does not define relocation reimbursement.

ESCALATION
If a question concerns a policy not in this handbook, ask the HR policy owner. Do not invent eligibility, exceptions, approval or reimbursement amounts.
